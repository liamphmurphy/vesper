Shortcuts

Global shortcuts that work from any app. Click a field and press the keys; use ⌘, ⌥ or ⌃ (Shift alone isn't enough).

Quick Capture Capture a note, message a persona, or file a ticket.
Screenshot to Chat Drag to capture part of the screen (Space for a window, Esc to cancel), then pick a chat to send it to. macOS asks for Screen Recording permission the first time.
Show or hide Vesper Brings Vesper forward from any app, or hides it when it's already in front.

Files & Links

Send files dropped on Vesper to a chat Drop files on the Dock icon, on a Vesper window, or use Open With. You pick the chat first. PDF, images, text, Markdown, CSV, JSON or zip, up to 25 MB each.
"Send to Vesper" Quick Actions Adds Send to Vesper (files, in Finder) and Send Text to Vesper (selected text) to the Services menu and Finder's Quick Actions.
Open vesper:// links vesper://run/<id>, vesper://ticket/<KEY-1>, vesper://epic/<id>, vesper://page/<id>, vesper://chat/<id>, vesper://inbox and vesper://capture. Anything else is ignored.

Updates

Vesper.app isn't notarised, so it can't update itself. It can tell you when a newer DMG is out.

Check for new versions daily Asks the release feed (GitHub) once a day. Nothing about you or your workspace is sent.
Updating Download the new DMG and drag Vesper.app into Applications, replacing the old one. Your data stays in the data folder and is upgraded on next launch.
